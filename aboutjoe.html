Joe Bart

Joseph "Joe" Bartolozzi (born February 6, 2002) is an American Tiktoker, YouTuber, and streamer, best known for his high-energy comedic videos on TikTok and YouTube. He rose to fame in the early 2020s with his commentary and reaction videos, often characterized by his expressive rants and humorous takes on internet trends. He's also known for gaming, reactions and vlogs. He won an award in 2024 for "Best Variety Streamer". As of Late 2025, Bartolozzi has roughly 24.7 Million TikTok Followers and 3.3 Million YouTube Subscribers. However he has expressed in the past that he values YouTube subscribers over TikTok followers.

Early Life

Bartolozzi was born and raised in New Jersey. He is ethnically Italian. He participated in athletics during high school, mainly track and field. Prior to his social media fame, he was active in sports and considered a potential collegiate athlete. He graduated magna cum laude from Stockton University in New Jersey with a bachelor's in arts, philosophy, and religion.

His "Death"

Around June of 2023, a hoax began circulating on TikTok that he had died. There are also persistent rumors alleging that he died in the hills, rumors that originate on February 6, 2002, and continue to circulate to the present day. This started one of his biggest jokes that he had been in the hills and a song going by "I'VE BEEN IN THE HILLS" by Ariis started surging in popularity. which is about joe being in the hills
